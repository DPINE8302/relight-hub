# RE:Light Mini Games

Standalone Thai-first collection of three original web experiments about e-cigarettes. Audience: Thai teenagers, young adults, exhibition visitors, and anyone curious. The site works without visiting the main RE:Light site or film.

The visitor chooses a game, plays immediately, discovers a specific insight, and optionally reads evidence. Competition alignment: เท่ ดูดีได้โดยไม่สูบบุหรี่ไฟฟ้า. No attractiveness score, blame, diagnosis, assumed future success, or quantitative health simulation.

Approved visual authority: ../output/imagegen/RELight_Mini_Games_Visual_Concept_v1.png. Warm ivory gallery, chunky Thai type, yellow creative studio, lavender inspection cabinet, coral language laboratory. Original imagegen art; controls and Thai lettering live in code. Product mode: Experience.

Games: scene composition and ad comparison; fictional object inspection; claim-versus-evidence testing. Phone, desktop, keyboard, reduced motion, mute, reload recovery, real local scene export. No accounts or public uploads. No private-response analytics. Existing film/site remain separate.

Current scope derives from the user's approved discussion, visual board, GDD, and active build objective. There is no new visual-direction decision to solicit. Implementation can refine layouts for actual devices while preserving the board's world.
