# PUFF//WORLD: INSIDE — portable browser edition

Double-click **Start Game.command** on a Mac with Python 3. Keep its Terminal window open while playing. Open http://127.0.0.1:8787/ if the browser does not open automatically. No internet connection or npm installation is needed.

Move with WASD, jump with Space, and click / Q / E to breathe. Drag to look around. Esc opens pause/settings. J opens help. Shift sprints; holding Space while falling glides.

Thai is the default. EN / ไทย switches language; story captions display one language at a time. LINE Seed Sans TH is bundled for offline play. Narration is currently text only.

Watch or skip the opening and each area's educational scene. Clear twelve particles, jump across four heart platforms, then follow the blue route out of the brain while pushing the golden lure away. Finally, walk into a portal to choose an ending.

This archive contains the compiled game and launcher, including five adapted Quaternius nature models. The editable source, references and QA evidence stay in the original project folder. Asset licenses are included; see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.
