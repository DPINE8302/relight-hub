# RE:Light Arcade v2

Recovery build after the user rejected the original three games and approved the attached five-experiment direction. Preserve the old project; this project is independent. Thai-first, standalone, mobile and keyboard accessible. No account, telemetry or uploaded responses. Sound is optional; motion reduction and immediate exit are always available.

Experience mode. Five concepts: ONE MORE HIT, BUILD YOUR COOL, WHAT'S IN THE CLOUD?, KEEP ME ALIVE, THE ALGORITHM. The attachment supplies clean contemporary graphic direction, short exploratory loops and an emphasis on addiction/marketing systems rather than blaming people who vape. The previous illustrated concept board is not a promise for this mechanics prototype.

Every game needs player-controlled state, consequential feedback, a discoverable contrast and a specific useful takeaway. A technical pass is not evidence of learning. Clearly label authored models; no invented health numbers, deterministic per-puff physiological outcomes or claims of real product testing. Sources checked 6 Oct 2026: CDC Health Effects of Vaping, CDC About E-Cigarettes, WHO Tobacco E-cigarettes.
