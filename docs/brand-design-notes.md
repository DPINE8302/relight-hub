# Identity guide refinement — 13 October 2026

Surface: docs/brand/index.html. Mode: Experience with immediate asset retrieval. Preserve the established RE;light identity: navy, ivory, gold, LINE Seed Sans TH, approved doorway/path and gold semicolon.

The first view is the identity itself: wordmark, artwork, locked Thai message and three primary swatches. Asset exports live directly beside previews. ## Reference evidence

- NASA, Graphic Standards: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/brand-guidelines/ — shows identifiers and application rules together. Transfer: reusable assets with production constraints.
- Spotify, Design & Branding Guidelines: https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design — full-logo versus compact-icon roles and clear-space examples. Transfer: previews beside the correct download, rather than explanatory paragraphs.
- Mozilla, Protocol brand themes: https://protocol.mozilla.org/docs/fundamentals/brand-themes — shared tokens across distinct brand themes. Transfer: a small core palette and secondary technical tokens.
- Pentagram / Paula Scher, The Public Theater: https://www.pentagram.com/work/the-public-theater — typography carries identity across cultural communications. Transfer: Thai/Latin specimens and coherent applications without copying the theater's type or layout.

Accessed 13 October 2026. Direction is synthesized from these references and existing RE;light assets; no third-party artwork is reused. Some candidate COLLINS/Pentagram project pages were inaccessible and were excluded.

## Decisions

- Remove repeated introductory prose, equal-weight eight-color grid, rejected-asset history and source bookkeeping from the visible page.
- Keep support information in one native details disclosure and the downloadable README.
- Use real 400/700/800 font files; no synthetic weights. Natural tracking for Thai; 1.5–1.8 leading for marks and tone vowels.
- Add outlined SVGs and transparent 2400px PNGs while retaining original live-text editable masters.
- Distribute unmodified desktop TTF and web WOFF2 copies from the official LINE package, with the OFL license.
- No claim of universal CMYK equivalence, room acceptance or ten-second human comprehension. Print requires a printer-profile proof; viewing-distance typography requires exhibition testing.
