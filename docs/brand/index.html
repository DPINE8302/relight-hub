Application studies

Production notes & supporting colors

Logo & artwork

  • Use the supplied wordmarks. Keep the gold semicolon.
  • Clear space: at least the height of the lowercase “l”.
  • Use the compact mark for icons; the illustration for larger compositions.
  • SVG logos are outlined. PNG logos have transparent backgrounds.
  • Keep artwork proportions; never stretch or add effects.

Type & contrast

  • Thai tracking: 0. Allow room for vowels and tone marks.
  • Web body: 16–18 px, line height 1.65–1.8. Display: 32–64 px.
  • Use navy text on ivory; ivory or gold on navy. Keep gold off light body text.
  • Film: place ivory subtitles on a dark backing; check from the actual viewing distance.

Print & motion

Use SVG for scalable logos. Check raster artwork at final size. These are RGB masters: proof colors in the printer’s ICC profile. For motion, reveal the existing doorway or path slowly; keep reading text still.
